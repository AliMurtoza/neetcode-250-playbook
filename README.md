# NeetCode 250 Playbook

A structured, pattern-first journey through the NeetCode 250.

This repository documents the process of solving all 250 problems while building a reusable understanding of algorithmic patterns, problem-solving techniques, and recognition rules.

## What is this project?

This is more than a collection of 250 solutions.

For every problem, the goal is to document:

- What the problem is asking
- Initial intuition
- Possible brute-force or naive approaches
- Why a better approach is needed
- The key observation
- The algorithm, data structure, or pattern used
- The C++ implementation
- Why the solution works
- Time and space complexity
- A reusable recognition rule
- The main takeaway

The focus is on understanding **how to arrive at a solution**, not just recording the final code.

## The Playbook Philosophy

Individual problems are examples.

The real goal is to identify the patterns connecting those problems and turn them into reusable problem-solving knowledge.
